More picture.
Less fuss.

A little control over how you watch.

Make room for your video

Play a video on YouTube, Netflix or Disney+, then open Fillframe from the browser toolbar. Choose Fill player, choose the picture ratio when black bars are encoded inside the video, or adjust extra zoom, and move the picture until it feels right.

Filling a frame can crop the picture. Original brings back the original framing. Reset framing clears your adjustments.

Toggle Original / Fill playerAlt + Shift + F

On Mac, Alt is the Option key. The shortcut works while the video page has focus, outside text fields.

Private by design

Your remembered framing preferences stay in this browser’s local extension storage. There is no account, subscription, paywall or analytics service.

Fillframe adjusts the video on the page. It does not download videos or remove a streaming service’s subscription requirements.

If the picture does not change

Make sure a video is playing. Refresh tabs that were open before installation, then reopen Fillframe. Website players change, so a particular player or fullscreen mode may need a compatibility fix.

This build targets desktop Chromium browsers. Compatibility must be checked in each browser and on each service; it is not a guarantee for every video.
